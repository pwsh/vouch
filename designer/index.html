Waiting for you to click in the browser…

Press Esc in the browser to cancel.

Keys do nothing on the page while you pick: Esc cancels; the arrow keys, Page Up/Down and Space scroll. To work with the keyboard instead, cancel and type the words or selector in the Designer.

Menu items that only appear while the pointer is over a menu cannot be picked, and a run could not reach them either: look for another way to that page, often its own address.

The page does not get your clicks or keys while you pick, with one exception: a page that reacts to a click anywhere (for example “click anywhere to close”) may still notice yours.
